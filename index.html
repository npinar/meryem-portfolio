About Me

Tennis isn’t just something I play, it’s something I love. From the first ball to the last point, I’m happiest when I’m on the court, competing and working to get better.

I’ve learned that every match teaches me something, whether I win or lose. A tough loss shows me what to fix, and a big win reminds me why the hard work matters. I stay patient through long rallies, keep my focus when the score gets tight, and always try to bring good energy to the court.

Off the court, I’m just as committed. I work hard in school, stay disciplined with my training, and value the friends, coaches, and family who support me along the way.

My goal is to play tennis in college, and I’m putting in the work every day to get there. This site is a record of that journey. Thanks for stopping by, and I hope you’ll follow along.
